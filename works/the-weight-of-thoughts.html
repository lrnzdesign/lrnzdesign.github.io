The weight of thoughts

The video explores mental distance through abstract graphics and is divided into three parts:

the first sequence visually translates the process of cognitive fusion. The objective is to demonstrate how a single mental element can expand until it occupies the entire field of attention, obscuring everything else.

In the second phase, the video moves away from the static nature of the block to explore dynamism. We are not yet seeking silence, but we begin to distinguish the individual elements that previously appeared as an impenetrable wall. In the concluding sequence, the geometric shapes (the thoughts) collide head-on. It is no longer the chaotic coexistence of the first part, but a process of visual catharsis that cuts thoughts down to size.

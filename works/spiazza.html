Spiazza

Spiazza brings Rome's lesser-known squares to life, transforming them into spaces for social interaction, gathering, and memory.

The square becomes a symbol of connection and collective identity: a place where different stories meet and intertwine. The logo is a stylized map of the neighborhood around the square. The empty center is an invitation for people to fill the space with life. Since every square is different, the project uses a color-coded system. Five specific colors represent five categories of activities, making the identity of each square immediately clear.
